# Risk Profiles

## What is a Risk Profile?
A risk profile describes how much investment risk a person is willing and able to take on, based on their financial situation, time horizon, and psychological tolerance for seeing their portfolio value fluctuate. Finvest AI classifies users into three broad risk profiles: conservative, moderate, and aggressive.

## Conservative Investors
Conservative investors prioritize capital preservation over growth. They typically hold a higher proportion of bonds and cash, often 70-80% fixed income and cash versus 20-30% equities. This profile suits investors nearing retirement, those with a short time horizon, or those who would be significantly stressed by a large short-term portfolio decline.

## Moderate Investors
Moderate investors seek a balance between growth and stability. A common moderate allocation is roughly 60% equities and 40% bonds, sometimes called a "60/40 portfolio." This profile suits investors with a medium-to-long time horizon, typically 10-20 years, who can tolerate moderate volatility in exchange for better long-term growth than a conservative portfolio.

## Aggressive Investors
Aggressive investors prioritize long-term growth and can tolerate significant short-term volatility, including drawdowns of 30% or more in a severe downturn. Allocations are often 85-100% equities, sometimes including a higher weighting toward growth stocks or emerging markets. This profile suits investors with a long time horizon, typically 20+ years, such as younger investors saving for retirement.

## Risk Capacity vs Risk Tolerance
Risk capacity is the objective ability to withstand losses based on income, savings, and time horizon. Risk tolerance is the subjective, psychological comfort with volatility. An investor may have high risk capacity but low risk tolerance, or vice versa. A sound recommendation considers both, not just one.

## Reassessing Risk Profile
A user's risk profile is not fixed. It should be reassessed whenever there is a major life event, such as a new job, marriage, the birth of a child, nearing retirement, or a significant change in income. Finvest AI should recommend a profile review roughly once a year or after any major life change the user mentions.
